The Mission

Energy, hardware, education, and community aren't separate businesses here — they're one system, engineered by someone who has never separated her own disciplines either.

Morenita Technology exists to prove that range is a structural advantage, not a distraction, and to build the infrastructure most companies won't: the kind that has to work across all four at once.

House of
Morenita

I'm designing a self-powered campus housing clean energy generation, data centers, and net zero print and clothing manufacturing — all in one place, built to replicate.

Wind and solar generation power it, and I share the surplus with the surrounding community. The first campus proves the blueprint; each one after it is modular and replicable.

Belongs to Pillar 01 — Self-Generating Energy

Solar
Fabrics

I'm researching photovoltaic textiles — weaving solar cells directly into fabric, for wearable and architectural energy generation, instead of bolting a rigid panel onto something else.

Early-stage — a natural extension of House of Morenita's manufacturing and my own textile and painting practice. I'm still scoping the direction; this isn't a finished result yet.

The
Digibook

I'm building a physical book with 30 paper-like screens, powered by a projector in the spine. It shows a personalized weekly magazine — curated around art, science, nature, and creativity. Write and sketch on it with a custom stylus.

Solar-powered case, recycled components, low-energy architecture. Everything saves to Library of Morenita automatically.

Division 05
Prototyped
Belongs to Pillar 02 — Systems That Absorb the Burden

MAXINE

A healthcare recovery companion device that assists nurses and caretakers by streamlining patient tracking, medication administration, and bookkeeping across multiple patients simultaneously.

Nurse burnout is one of the leading crises in healthcare — the administrative burden, not the clinical work, is the primary driver. MAXINE removes it.

⊢ PATIENT TRACKING ⊣ ⊢ MEDICATION LOG ⊣ ⊢ AUTOMATED RECORDS ⊣

Signals
